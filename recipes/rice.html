Jollof Rice Recipes

Description

Below are the ingredients for a Nigerian Jollof Rice

  • Long grain parboiled rice
  • Fesh Tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Onions
  • Vegetable oil
  • Tomato paste
  • Seasonings
  • Proteins

Steps

Here’s a step-by-step guide to preparing a Nigerian Jollof Rice

  1. Prepare the meat and stock
  2. Make the pepper base (blend)
  3. Fry the tomato paste
  4. Cook the pepper mix
  5. Season the sauce
  6. Add stock and rice
  7. Cook the jollof rice
  8. Achieve smoky flavor
  9. Add vegetables and rest
  10. Serve
