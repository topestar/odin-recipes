Yam Porridge Recipes

Description

Below are the ingredients for a Nigerian Yam Porridge meal

  • 1 medium tuber of yam
  • Palm oil
  • Chopped Onions
  • Fish
  • Scotch bonnet peppers
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Peppers & Vegetables
  • Water

Steps

Here’s a step-by-step guide to preparing a Nigerian beans Meal

  1. Prepare the yam
  2. Parboil the yam
  3. Prepare the fish
  4. Add palm oil and peppers
  5. Cook and mash the yam
  6. Add fish and vegetables
  7. Final taste
  8. Serve
